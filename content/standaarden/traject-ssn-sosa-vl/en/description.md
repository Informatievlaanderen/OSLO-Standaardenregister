<p>The SSN SOSA VL model, in full the Flemish Standard for sensor, observation, sampling and actuator, provides a standardized framework for managing and exchanging sensor, observation, sampling and actuator data in Flanders. The focus of this model is on the effective representation and application of international standards within the broad theme of measurement, monitoring, sampling and automation. The model is closely aligned with the W3C/OGC model SSN SOSA and is aligned with complementary models such as PROV-O, QUDT, and where relevant p-plan and GeoSPARQL. Insights from previous OSLO projects, such as OSLO Soil and Subsurface, Water Quality and Erosion Pool, are also integrated to ensure a coherent Flemish interpretation.</p>



<p>The model classifies the different types of entities that are relevant for sensors, observations, sampling and actuators, as defined in the underlying international SSN SOSA framework. In addition, the model facilitates the integration of semantic and procedural references, including design principles, best practices and agreements on modelling. This creates an unambiguous and well-documented standard for recording data within various domains.</p>



<p>The data model is the central reference point for the description and structuring of observations, measurements, sampling, systems, procedures and actuators. It supports different types of data sources, such as sensor networks, field measurements, laboratory analyses and automated measurement systems. Within this framework, various model elements are distinguished, including Observations, Samples, Sensors, Actuators and Procedures. These elements make it possible to structure and exchange data in a uniform and interpretable way.</p>



<p>The model also makes it possible to analyse relationships between observations and their context in detail. Through links with PROV-O and QUDT, among others, origin, procedures, units, uncertainties and quality aspects can be accurately recorded. This is essential for assessing reliability, reproducibility and comparability of measurement data in a variety of applications.</p>



<p>Furthermore, the model provides a clear way to document the evolution, implementation and procedural steps around observations and sampling. Key stages such as data collection, validation, quality control and processing can be formally described, allowing for transparent and complete documentation of the methods and processes used.</p>



<p>The SSN SOSA VL model is widely supported and meets the needs of Flemish organisations that work with measurement and observation data. The model supports the development of unambiguous data examples, code lists and agreements that contribute to a consistent and interoperable data exchange within Flanders.</p>
