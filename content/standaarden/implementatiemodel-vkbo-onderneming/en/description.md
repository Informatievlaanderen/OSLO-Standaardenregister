The Implementation Model VKBO - Enterprise applies OSLO Organization to the Enhanced Crossroads Bank for Enterprises (VKBO) dataset of Digital Flanders
an interoperable and standardised approach. Specific terms specific to the VKBO have been included in a separate vocabulary,
all other terms are taken from vocabularies of OSLO Organization and the Organization Ontology (W3C). OSLO Organization is aligned with the SEMIC
Core Public Organisation Vocabulary and the Core Business Vocabulary. Only public information about companies is currently included in the implementation model.
